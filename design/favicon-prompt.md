# EX NIHILO favicon

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The luminous seed and violet
spiral connect the opening singularity to the later galaxies. The dark background
preserves the silhouette in both light and dark browser chrome.

Original: `favicon-source.png` in this directory.
Website assets: `public/favicon.png` (512 px), 16 px and 32 px PNGs,
`public/favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 and 256 px), and a 180 px Apple touch icon.
The browser variants are downscaled from the generated original.
