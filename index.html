01
The molecular cloud

Nothing.
Then, almost.

In the cold dark, a cloud of gas and dust begins to gather. Gravity draws it inward. Somewhere inside, the future is getting warmer.

02
The protostar

A secret
catches fire.

The cloud contracts. A dense, hot heart takes shape. When the core becomes hot enough for hydrogen fusion, a star begins its long act of balancing.

MotionCollapseThresholdIgnition
03
The main sequence

To live
is to burn.

Gravity pulls inward. Pressure pushes outward. For most of its life, the star holds this beautiful tension, turning hydrogen into helium and light.

FuelHydrogenStateEquilibrium
04
The red supergiant

One last
great breath.

As hydrogen in the core runs out, the star changes. Its outer layers swell. Deep inside, heavier elements are forged as the end draws closer.

SurfaceCoolingEnvelopeExpanding
05
The core collapse

An ending
too bright
to forget.

The core can no longer support itself. It collapses, and a supernova sends the star's outer layers racing into space. What was one star becomes material for worlds.

The remnant

Gone.
Never nothing.

In this star's story, the remaining core is massive enough to become a black hole. The light is gone. Its gravity keeps writing the story.
