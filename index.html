ex nihilo /ˌeks ˈniː.hɪ.loʊ/ — Latin

out of nothing

Everything there is — every star, every atom, the screen you are reading this on — began as less than a point. No space around it. No time before it.

EX NIHILO compresses those first 13.8 billion years into a single scroll. Here, the scrollbar is time: drag it forward and you pull the universe into being — the Big Bang, the first light, the first stars, the slow turning of galaxies, and their long fall toward one another.

It is rendered live — light, sound, and mathematics, computed in the moment, never a video. The sound you hear is generated as you go; the clock in the corner counts real cosmic time.
